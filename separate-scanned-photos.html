Why Separating Scanned Photos in Bulk Saves Hours

Manually cropping photos one by one in desktop image editors takes hours when you have boxes of family photo albums to digitize. With PhotoSplit Studio, you simply place 4 to 6 photos across the scanner glass and scan them together as a single sheet.

Step-by-Step Guide for Optimal Results

  1. Leave space between photos: Keep at least 1/2 inch (1 cm) of white space between each print on the scanner glass so the detection algorithm identifies clear boundaries.
  2. Scan at 300 to 600 DPI: 300 DPI is perfect for standard 4x6 prints, while 600 DPI is recommended for small vintage prints or wallet photos.
  3. Auto-Straighten: Even if photos are placed slightly crooked, the built-in edge alignment corrects rotation automatically up to 30 degrees.
  4. One-Click Batch Export: Download all separated files as individual high-res JPEGs, PNGs, or a single combined PDF booklet.
